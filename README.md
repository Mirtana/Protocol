MirtanaProtocol - https://mirtana.github.io/Protocol/

MirtanaProtocol is a modern Web3 application with an intuitive interface, combining a wide range of DeFi and NFT mechanics.

The project is deployed and actively functions across four testnets: 
Arc Network, Robinhood Chain, KII Chain, and OPN Chain.

Currently, 10 smart contracts are deployed in the ecosystem, ensuring the seamless operation of all features.

🚀 Available Features
1. GM - Daily Activity
Maintain your network activity every 24 hours and record your presence on the blockchain.

2. Token Mint (MIRTA)
An opportunity to mint the protocol's primary token - MIRTA.

Arc Network: 0.0001 USDC

Robinhood Chain: 0.000001 ETH

KII Chain: 0.00001 KII

OPN Chain: 0.0001 OPN

3. Swap (Buyback)
Reverse swap of MIRTA tokens back to native network assets or stablecoins:

MIRTA ➔ USDC (Arc): 0.0001 USDC

MIRTA ➔ ETH (Robinhood): 0.0000009 ETH

MIRTA ➔ KII (Kii Chain): 0.00001 KII

MIRTA ➔ OPN (OPN Chain): 0.0001 OPN


4. Staking
Grow your holdings by choosing one of the four available staking plans:

Flexible: 10.5% APY

7 Days: 15% APY

30 Days: 18% APY

90 Days: 22.5% APY


5. Send MIRTA
Simple and fast transfer of MIRTA tokens between wallets within the supported networks.
Arc Network, Robinhood Chain, KII Chain, OPN Chain.

7. Token Generator (Token Factory)
Create your own tokens in just a few clicks. Three types of configurations are available:

Standard: A classic ERC-20 token.

Burnable: A token featuring a burn function.

Tax (5%): A token with a transaction fee commission.

7. NFT Mint & Dashboard
An exclusive collection of 12 NFTs, themed around the months of the year (from Mirtana January to Mirtana December).

Cost: 25,000 MIRTA per 1 NFT.

Features: On-chain liking system and a transfer function for each individual NFT.

Hall of Months: A personalized dashboard to showcase and view your collection.

8. On-Chain Liking System (NFT Likes)
An interactive mechanic for engaging with the collection.
Users can express their appreciation and leave likes under any of the 12 NFTs.
Every action is fully recorded and processed by smart contracts across the Arc Network, Robinhood Chain, KII Chain, and OPN Chain.

10. MIRTA ECHO (SocialFi)
A decentralized platform for communication among NFT holders.

Access: Requires holding at least 1 NFT from the MirtanaProtocol collection.

Post Cost: 10,000 MIRTA.

Mechanics: A post of up to 145 characters lives on the blockchain for 24 hours, after which it disappears.

Ranking System (Visual highlighting of posts):

1-5 NFTs: Rank: Loyal Custodian

6-11 NFTs: Rank: Epic Hero

12 NFTs: Rank: Legend of Myrtana
